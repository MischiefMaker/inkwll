## Patterns they share

- **Big themed category headings or banners** that group the boards beneath them. In Inkwll these map to top-level Areas, with the Areas or Settings inside shown as boards.
- **Each board row has an image or styled box, a short description, quick links to its sub-boards, and its latest activity** (last post, who, when) plus counts.
- **One strong colour plus a neutral**, with a decorative display font for headings and a plain font for body text.
- **A nav bar of the game's reference pages** near the top (Plot, Rules, App, Claims, FAQ…). In Inkwll these will be wiki pages in the Menu.
- **A news line and "welcome back"** under the header, and on some, the in-character date and weather.
- **A chat box** (Cbox) always on screen, which Inkwll already has as the OOC sidebar.
