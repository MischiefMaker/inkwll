## Goal

A roster: characters other players can apply to take over, with staff approval. Idle characters go back on the roster.

## Background

Owner decisions are under "Characters are retired, never deleted" and "Rosters" in `docs/decisions.md`. Retiring characters is already built (Admin → Characters).

## Questions for the owner (answered 2026-10-14; see `docs/decisions.md`)

1. When does a character count as idle: after a set time without a post or move (a game setting), when the player gives it up, when staff decide, or a mix?
2. Can a player put their own character on the roster directly?
3. What does an applicant fill in? This could reuse the Application tab and approval from task 0014.
4. When a character changes hands, what does the new player see: the old player's private notes, messages, relationships and unfinished Chapters?
5. Should past Manuscripts credit the old player, the new one, or neither? Player names are hidden by default.
6. Can staff add characters to the roster that no player owns yet, like pre-made or "wanted" characters?
