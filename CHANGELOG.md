# Changelog

Short, plain-language notes on what changed. Newest first.

## 2026-10-17 — Finding someone to RP with (Version 2.0 branch)

- Looking for RP: switch it on for a character (on their profile, or the new Looking for RP page in the Menu) with a short "I am…" and "Looking for…". The character gets a mark in Here and on Where, and shows on the Looking for RP page until you switch it off.

## 2026-10-17 — Quick wins (Version 2.0 branch)

- Drafts: what you're writing in a Passage, a Message, a forum post, a wiki page or an Inkling is kept in your browser as you type, so it comes back if the page closes or reloads. Posting clears it.
- Graveyard: staff can mark a character deceased (with an optional epitaph), which retires them and lists them on a new Graveyard page.
- Birthdays: a page in the Menu listing the characters whose birthday falls in the game's current month.
- A "New" mark beside new players (their first two weeks) in the OOC Chat, and beside their characters in Here and the Character Directory, so others know to say hello.
- Away notice: set "Away until" a date on your player profile, with a note, and it shows on your Chapters beside whose turn it is until that day has passed.
- Private Game: a setting under Admin → Game settings that sends anyone not logged in to the log-in page.
- New players can be made to wait for staff approval (another setting there). Staff approve or turn them away at the top of Players and staff.
- Admin → Look can put the Chapter tabs down the left-hand side instead of along the top (phones keep them on top).
- Tick boxes on Admin → Characters and Admin → Players and staff: tick several (or tick the heading for all), then retire them, put them on the roster, or archive the players in one go.

## 2026-10-17 — Items and Crafting (Version 2.0 branch)

- With the Economy on, every character has a Belongings tab that everyone can see. Players give items (or part of a stack) to other characters, and start a Trade where both sides put in money and items and it goes through once both tick Agree.
- Admin → Item catalogue: ready-made items staff sell in the shops of chosen Places, and give to characters with a reason. A character standing in a Place can buy from its shop. Inklings can reward a catalogue item.
- Crafting, switched on under Admin → Features: characters pay a starting cost plus each feature's cost, write up the item with pictures, and roll its Skill. A great roll makes a Masterwork, a complicated one adds a flaw, and a failure loses money. Made items carry their maker's mark.
- Item features count in rolls as tick boxes beside Boons and Banes, and the Storyteller can rule on them. Flaws always count.
- A Market page lists everything for sale. Sales count as income for faction income tax.
- Admin → Crafting: what can be made, features, players' feature proposals, and the costs and limits.

## 2026-10-17 — Domains (Version 2.0 branch)

- Land in a chain of lieges, such as Realm, Duchy, County and Barony, switched on under Admin → Features (it needs the Economy). Wellkeepers make them under Admin → Domains, with a holder, a number of people and a yield per head.
- On the 1st of each month every Domain earns from its people, pays its tax up the chain, and its holder keeps the rest in their Purse (or their faction's Bank).
- A Domains page in the menu shows the tree of land and who holds it. Holders see their earnings and set the tax their vassals pay them.
- An Area linked to a Domain says whose land it is.

## 2026-10-17 — The Economy (Version 2.0 branch)

- Money, switched on under Admin → Features. Every character has a Purse tab (seen only by their player and staff) with their balance and a ledger of every change.
- Pay any character or faction from the Purse, with a note, optionally inside a Chapter, where it shows as a short line. Set up scheduled payments every week, two weeks or month; the payer can stop them and the payee can turn them down.
- Factions get a Bank. Staff tick Bank managers, who can pay out, set up salaries, and (when taxes are on) set income tax and dues. Members can read the Bank.
- Admin → Economy: the money's name, a starting amount, a weekly allowance, Boons and Banes that change it, game payments, giving and taking money with a reason, and recent transactions.
- Inklings can give money as a reward.

## 2026-10-17 — Factions (Version 2.0 branch)

- Factions: families, guilds and clubs, and a character can be in several. Switch them on under Admin → Features, and make them under Admin → Factions.
- Players tick factions on their character's application. After approval, a faction's heads and staff add members from the faction's page, with an optional title such as "Apprentice".
- A faction can be secret, so only its members and staff know it exists, and one membership can be secret too.
- Staff choose heads. A faction can have NPC heads, and staff run any faction whose heads are only NPCs or on the roster.
- Approved characters can "Request to join" a faction that isn't secret, with an optional note. Its heads get an alert, staff see it waiting under the bell, and either can accept or decline it from the faction's page.
- Factions show under the character's name on their profile, and an Inkling can be shared with a whole faction.

## 2026-10-17 — The starting map has streets

- The sample map a new game starts with now has a street, lane or path in every smaller Area: Uptown, Midtown and Downtown Street in the City; High Street, Hedgerow Lane and The Towpath in the Country; Woodland Track, Mossy Path and Shoreline Trail in the Forest. The streets join north to south, and the buildings and outdoor spaces (Ashcombe Hotel, Central Square, the Trailhead and the rest) sit on them, so you can see Streets, Buildings and Outdoor areas working. Each Area's Enter button now lands on its street. Games that already have the sample places get the streets added too, once, when they update (nothing is changed or removed, and a game with a different map is left alone).

## 2026-10-17 — One step for a Storyteller's call for a roll

- "Call for a roll" now has the Boons and Banes drop-downs right in it (for whichever character you pick, every one starting as "Player's choice"). If nothing is left for the player to choose, the roll happens at once; otherwise it waits for the player to choose and roll.
- The "Roll with the Storyteller" button shows its name again instead of "1".

## 2026-10-17 — Choosing Boons and Banes on rolls

- The roll box lists the character's Boons and Banes as tick boxes. Picking a Skill ticks the ones that affect it; the player can untick them or tick others, then rolls, with no extra step.
- When the Storyteller judges a roll (asked for or called for), they set the difficulty and mark each Boon and Bane "Must count", "Player's choice" or "Doesn't count". The player then sees the required ones and ticks any optional ones, and rolls it themselves. A character with no Boons or Banes is still rolled straight away. This follows how the SOUL plugin handled GM-assisted rolls.

## 2026-10-17 — Storyteller rolls work properly

- "Roll with the Storyteller" now really asks: the roll waits for the Storyteller instead of rolling straight away.
- When the Storyteller calls for a roll, the waiting box says "was asked to roll" rather than "wants to roll".
- The Storyteller's box says so when the character has no Boons or Banes, instead of leaving the space blank.

## 2026-10-17 — A font box for big titles

- Admin → Look has a third font box, "Font for big titles", beside text and headings. The game's name on the front page and the titles of forum sections use it, so a script font like Great Vibes can be changed without touching CSS. Left empty, they use the heading font as before.

## 2026-10-17 — No fonts are built into Inkwll any more

- Great Vibes, Amatic SC and Oswald, the last fonts that came with Inkwll itself, are gone. Last Call of the Wild now brings its own copies in its world pack, as every game's fonts do.

## 2026-10-17 — Importing a look replaces the old one

- When a world pack has a look, Import a world now replaces the game's whole look with it. Before, bits of the earlier look stayed behind: with the Old-school MUSH look, the pack's pages still showed "<OOC>" labels and square MUSH styling.

## 2026-10-16 — Update every Well skips what's already current

- "Update every Well" no longer redeploys a Well (including this game) that is already on that version.

## 2026-10-16 — A game's fonts travel with its world pack

- A world pack can now carry its own web fonts. Import a world stores them in the game and serves them from it, so a game's fonts no longer have to be built into Inkwll for everyone. Admin → Look lists them under the form, with a Remove button for each, and you can name their families in the font boxes as before.

## 2026-10-16 — Wiki bullets take their group's colour

- On wiki pages, a bullet that names a character group (such as "15, male, Ravenclaw") is shown in that group's colour, bullet and all. Change a group's colour in Admin → Character groups and the wiki follows.

## 2026-10-16 — Import a world: nothing gets left behind

- With "Also remove what isn't in the pack" ticked, Places with characters or Chapters in them no longer stay behind. The characters move to The Prologue and the Chapters to a new Place, The Well of Lost Plots, in Out of Character, then the Place is removed. The preview says what will move where.

## 2026-10-16 — Import a world

- New Founder-only **Admin → Import a world** page. Upload a world pack (a file holding Areas, Places, Paths, wiki pages, boards, the front page text and the game's look) and it shows everything it would add, change or remove before anything happens. Importing the same pack again changes nothing. There's an option to also remove Areas, Places and Paths the pack leaves out; The Prologue and any Place with characters or Chapters in it always stay. Details in docs/world-packs.md.
- Three web fonts now come with Inkwll and can be named in Admin → Look: "Great Vibes" (flowing script), "Amatic SC" (hand-drawn capitals) and Oswald (tall and narrow). The Look page no longer suggests Google Fonts, which the Content Security Policy blocks.

- SOUL sheet: the Aspect "+0 to its Skills" column is gone; hover an Aspect's name for the bonus.
- The profile page uses more of a wide screen.
- A shield now marks things only you and staff can see; a lock marks fields that can't be edited any more (application answers after approval).

- The Cast a spell box in a Chapter's side column fits.
- "Add to an Inkling" in a Chapter (also in the Archives) asks why in a pop-up and adds an entry to the Inkling with a link to the Chapter. You can also cast a Grimoire spell in an Inkling.
